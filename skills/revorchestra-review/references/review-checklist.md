# Review Checklist

Use this checklist for every outbound draft review. Check each item and note pass/fail.

## Pre-Review Setup
- [ ] Research brief is available and readable
- [ ] Outbound draft is available and readable
- [ ] Channel type identified (email / linkedin / short_form)

## Hallucination Detection
- [ ] Company name is spelled correctly
- [ ] Company industry matches research brief
- [ ] Employee count / revenue claims match brief (if mentioned)
- [ ] Named personnel hold the stated titles (per brief)
- [ ] Trigger events actually appear in the research brief
- [ ] Trigger event dates are correct
- [ ] No metrics or statistics are fabricated
- [ ] No case studies or customer names are invented
- [ ] No competitor claims that aren't in the brief

## Messaging Rule Compliance

### Email Checks
- [ ] Subject line under 8 words
- [ ] Subject line is specific (not generic clickbait)
- [ ] Opening line references something real and specific
- [ ] Opening line is NOT "Hope this finds you well" or equivalent
- [ ] Body is 3–5 sentences
- [ ] Total word count under 150
- [ ] Single, clear CTA
- [ ] CTA is low-friction (question format preferred)
- [ ] No inspirational quotes in signature

### LinkedIn Checks
- [ ] InMail is 2–3 sentences
- [ ] Connection note under 300 characters
- [ ] No product pitch in connection request
- [ ] Tone is conversational

### Universal Checks
- [ ] Company name used naturally (not just mail-merged)
- [ ] At least one company-specific detail from research
- [ ] Persona-appropriate language and pain points
- [ ] No forbidden elements (fabricated metrics, competitor badmouthing, assumptions stated as facts)
- [ ] No unfilled template tokens ([brackets], {braces})

## Tone Assessment
- [ ] Sounds like a sharp colleague, not a salesperson
- [ ] Confident but not arrogant
- [ ] Specific and grounded
- [ ] Direct — point made in first sentence
- [ ] Not overly casual or emoji-heavy
- [ ] Not corporate jargon heavy

## Risk Scan
- [ ] No promises Taxxa can't keep
- [ ] No references to sensitive topics (layoffs, legal issues, financials) unless appropriate
- [ ] Message would not be perceived as spam
- [ ] No regulatory or compliance concerns
- [ ] Sending this would not embarrass Taxxa

## Final Decision
- Total weighted score: ___
- Decision: approve / revise / reject
- Key feedback (if revise/reject): ___
