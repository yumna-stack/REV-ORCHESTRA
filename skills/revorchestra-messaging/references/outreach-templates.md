# Outreach Templates

These are structural templates — not fill-in-the-blank scripts. Every message must be rewritten with genuine personalization from the research brief.

## Email Template Structure

```
Subject: {specific reference to their situation, under 8 words}

{First name},

{Opening: 1 sentence referencing a specific trigger event, pain point, or company detail from research. Must be verifiable.}

{Bridge: 1-2 sentences connecting their situation to Taxxa's value. Use persona-appropriate language.}

{Value: 1 sentence with a specific, concrete benefit. Not generic "save time and money."}

{CTA: 1 sentence, low-friction ask. Question format preferred.}

{Sign-off}
```

### Example (for illustration — never copy verbatim):

```
Subject: Tax complexity after your Series C

Sarah,

Congrats on the $45M raise — expanding into 12 new states is exciting, but it means 12 new tax jurisdictions to manage before Q4 filings.

We built Taxxa specifically for companies at your stage — when multi-state compliance grows faster than the finance team. Our clients at similar scale typically discover 15-20% in unclaimed credits during onboarding.

Worth a 15-minute call to see if that applies to {company}?

Best,
{name}
```

## LinkedIn InMail Template Structure

```
{First name} — {1 sentence hook tied to their specific role/situation}.

{1-2 sentences on how Taxxa relates to their context. Conversational tone.}

{CTA: question format, very low friction.}
```

## LinkedIn Connection Note Structure

```
{First name}, {specific reason for connecting — shared context, trigger event, or mutual interest}. Would love to exchange notes on {relevant topic}.
```

## Follow-Up Template Structure (Email)

```
Subject: Re: {original subject}

{First name},

{1 sentence referencing the original outreach + 1 new piece of value or context.}

{CTA: slightly different angle than the first ask.}
```

## Anti-Patterns (Never Do This)

- "Hope this finds you well" — generic, wastes the opening line
- "I noticed you're the VP of Finance at {company}" — yes, they know that
- "Companies like yours" without specifying what makes them similar
- "I'd love to pick your brain" — one-sided value proposition
- "[First Name]" or "{Company}" left as unfilled tokens
- Multiple CTAs in one message
- Attaching decks or PDFs in cold outreach
