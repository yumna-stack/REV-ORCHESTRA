# Paperclip

Open-source platform that orchestrates teams of AI agents as companies. The most literal interpretation of the [[Agent Economy]] vision.

## Key Features
- Org charts with hierarchies, roles, and reporting lines
- Per-agent monthly budgets with auto-pause at limits
- Ticket-based task management with audit trails
- Multi-company support with data isolation
- Governance layer (users operate as the board)
- Supports any agent runtime (Claude, Codex, Cursor, etc.)
